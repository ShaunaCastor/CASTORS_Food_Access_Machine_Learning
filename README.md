# CASTORS Research Initiative
### Machine Learning | Geospatial Analytics | AI Evaluation

**Research Lead:** Shauna Freeman Castor, Ph.D.  
**Study Area:** Trumbull County, Ohio  
**Status:** Ongoing Research | 2026

## Overview

The CASTORS Research Initiative uses machine learning and geospatial analytics to investigate relationships between socioeconomic conditions, transportation access, and food assistance participation.

The project demonstrates the development and evaluation of an AI-enabled analytical capability, from research design and data integration through model testing, validation, and refinement.

## Research Leadership & Technical Approach

- **Analytical Framework Development:** Defined research objectives, selected variables, and established model evaluation criteria.
- **Data Integration:** Combined Census, USDA, and EPA datasets using ArcGIS spatial analysis.
- **Machine Learning:** Developed Decision Tree and Random Forest models using AI-assisted Python coding.
- **Model Evaluation:** Assessed predictive performance using cross-validation, MAE, RMSE, and R².
- **Responsible AI:** Examined data quality, interpretability, model limitations, and generalizability.

Generative AI assisted with code development and troubleshooting. I directed the analytical approach, executed and tested models, interpreted results, and identified improvements.

## Tools & Methods

**Python | scikit-learn | pandas | NumPy | ArcGIS | Decision Trees | Random Forests | Model Evaluation**

## Research Status

Ongoing work includes refining predictors, incorporating additional socioeconomic indicators, expanding geographic coverage, and strengthening model validation.

The models are exploratory and are not intended for individual eligibility or automated decision-making.
